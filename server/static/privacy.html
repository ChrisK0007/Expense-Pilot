Privacy & data

Expense Pilot is a self-hosted expense manager. Your server administrator operates this installation and controls access, backups and optional integrations. Contact them with privacy or deletion questions.

Your records

Accounts, invoices, financial records, receipt originals and uploaded trip routes are stored on the configured server. They are accessible to the owner and authorised tax-agent accounts. Expense Pilot does not operate a central account or financial-data service. Server, reverse-proxy and hosting logs may record IP addresses and request metadata. Your administrator determines their retention.

Saved drive addresses sync between devices signed in to the same account and server. They are private to that login within the app; server administrators and backup holders can still access the database. Deletions sync to other devices when they next connect.

Receipt AI

No AI mode sends no receipts to an AI service. Local Ollama processes selected receipts on your companion service. Existing/remote Ollama sends them to the server selected by the administrator. Cloud AI sends receipt text and images to the selected OpenAI or Anthropic API when you request extraction. Provider charges, locations and retention policies apply. Expense Pilot does not automatically switch providers. AI output is a draft for your review.

API and SMTP credentials are encrypted on the server using its stored signing key. Financial records and receipt files are not encrypted by the application at rest; protect the host disk and backup archives. HTTPS protects data in transit when configured.

iPhone permissions and local drafts

Camera/photo/file access is used to capture receipts. Location and motion access support opt-in drive recording. Background location continues while recording or automatic tracking is enabled; stop or disable tracking to end monitoring. Car connection filtering reads the active audio route. Apple Maps and address lookup contact Apple when used. Face ID/Touch ID authenticates locally through iOS; the app does not receive biometric data.

Offline drafts are stored in protected app storage, tied to their original server and account, and excluded from device backups. Reviewed trips are uploaded with their route and removed locally only after the server confirms storage. Failed or uncertain uploads remain for retry. Removing the app or clearing this device deletes local-only drafts. Other device copies and exported files must be managed separately.

Other optional services

Configured SMTP servers deliver emails and optional BCC copies. PocketSmith supplies linked bank data when enabled. Each service has its own privacy policy. Contact your server administrator to identify enabled services and destinations.

Export and deletion

Settings provides expense export, owner backup downloads and account deletion. Deleting an owner account deletes the whole shared workspace. Deleting a tax-agent login does not delete the owner's financial records or activity history. Deleted records can still exist in earlier backups, exports, emails or other devices until those copies are removed by their holders. Keep any records you need before deleting them.

Supporting documents

Uploaded tax returns, health statements and other supporting documents are stored on this server separately from expenses. They are available to the owner and authorised tax agents, included in full backups, and are not sent to receipt AI services. Deleting a document removes its active original; existing backups and previously downloaded copies remain.
